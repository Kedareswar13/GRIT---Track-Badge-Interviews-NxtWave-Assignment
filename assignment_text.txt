AI Product Engineer: Round 1 Assignment

Context

GRIT is a year-long skilling competition for engineering students at NIAT. Students clear skill contests, and
once they have cleared every skill a Track Badge needs, they take a live online interview to earn that
badge. A small team of interviewers runs these interviews, each with their own availability. Their badge
gets nullified if the student was caught doing malpractice and got flagged later.

You can explore GRIT Website and How GRIT Works page for reference.

The problem

Design an Interview scheduling feature where:
    · Eligible students book an interview slot from their dashboard.
    · Interviewers set and manage their availability.
    · The interview team records the outcome: pass, fail, or retake later.
    · Students see their interview status and result on their dashboard.
    · The interview team sees which eligible students have booked and which haven't.

What to submit

Write up of (2 pages max)
    1. States: every state a student's interview journey can be in, and what moves it from one to the
         next. A simple table or diagram works.
    2. Edge cases and questions: the cases you would handle, and the assumptions
    3. Data model: your tables, key fields and schema, etc.

Working Prototype: Build with any AI vibe coding tool.
    4. A working prototype: Different screens that involve such as a student booking screen, and an
         interviewer's daily schedule with outcome recording, etc.
    5. Student messages: the 3 most important messages a student sees in this flow, written exactly as
         they would appear.
    6. Before and after screens: for the student home screen and other screens where you think this
         interview result will make a difference, show how it looks before and after the result. Build these
         into your working prototype.

A Loom Recording of you explaining the project in your own words & your thought process behind it.
